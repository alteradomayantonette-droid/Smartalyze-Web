## 1. DNSC RDE Agenda 2026-2030 — "DNSC SAILS"

**DNSC SAILS** = Davao del Norte State College: **S**ustaining gains in **A**gri-Aqua to accelerate **I**nnovation and **I**nclusion **L**eading to **S**ecured Futures. Five strategic priorities:

| Priority | Focus | Relevance to Smartalyze |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Sustain (Sustainability Agenda) | Biodiversity, nature-based solutions, SDG-focused education | Weak/indirect — mention only if framing Smartalyze as an SDG-4 (education) tool |
| 2. Accelerate Agri-Aqua (A3 Agenda) | Fishery management, agroforestry, value chain optimization | Weak/indirect — unless a future use case targets agri-data specifically |
| **3. Innovation and Inclusion (I2 Agenda)** | **ICT4D** (digital tools for grassroots development), food systems, GEDSI inclusion | **Strongest fit.** Smartalyze is explicitly an ICT4D tool: a digital platform lowering the technical barrier to data literacy for non-technical users (students, small business owners) |
| 4. Lead (Lead Agenda) | Just governance, niche industries (Blue Economy, Halal Industry, Banana Fibers/Textiles) | Weak/indirect |
| 5. Secured Futures (Security Agenda) | Resiliency, wellbeing, creative economy, future-proofing | Indirect — could tie in if framing data literacy as community resilience-building |

**What to write:** Smartalyze aligns most directly with **Strategic Priority 3 (Innovation and Inclusion / I2 Agenda)**, specifically its **ICT4D** component — "utilizing digital tools for grassroots development." Smartalyze operationalizes this by giving non-technical users (students, researchers, small business owners in Davao del Norte) a guided, no-code platform for data cleaning and analysis, directly supporting the RDE Office's mission to bridge "academic explorations and tangible community transformations" through locally-anchored digital innovation.

## 2. CHED A.C.H.I.E.V.E. Agenda 2025-2030

CHED's agenda has **4 thrusts + 3 enablers** (the "A.C.H.I.E.V.E." letters):

| Letter | Thrust/Enabler | Description | Relevance to Smartalyze |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Advanced and Accessible Lifelong Learning | Expand flexible, affordable, inclusive learning at every life stage | Moderate — Smartalyze supports informal skill-building in data literacy |
| **C** | Centralized One-Nation Human Capital Development | Align curricula/skills with labor market needs | Moderate — builds practical data-analysis skills relevant to employability |
| **H** | **Harmonized SDG-Based Research and Innovation** (enabler) | Research that contributes to national progress and the SDGs | **Strong fit** — Smartalyze is itself a research/innovation output supporting SDG-aligned work |
| **I** | **Integrated Real-Time Data Collection and Analytics System** (enabler) | "Using timely, accurate data to guide policies, planning, and decision-making" | **Strongest fit** — this is almost a literal description of what Smartalyze does at the institutional/individual level |
| E | Expanded and Impact-Driven Internationalization Strategies | Global academic partnerships, mobility | Not applicable |
| V | Vitalized Policies, Internal Systems, and Governance | Efficient, transparent, accountable governance | Indirect — data-driven decision-making culture |
| E | Effective and Efficient Public Service | Timely, responsive, people-centered service delivery | Indirect |

**What to write:** Smartalyze aligns with two CHED A.C.H.I.E.V.E. enablers in particular:
- **"I" — Integrated Real-Time Data Collection and Analytics**: Smartalyze directly supports this enabler's goal of "using timely, accurate data to guide policies, planning, and decision-making" by giving users (including HEI students and researchers) the tools to clean, analyze, and interpret their own data without requiring specialized technical training.
- **"H" — Harmonized SDG-Based Research and Innovation**: as a locally developed research output, Smartalyze itself represents applied research and innovation in service of accessible data science — consistent with CHED's call to "foster research and innovation that address real-world challenges."

## 3. DOST Harmonized National R&D Agenda (HNRDA) 2022-2028

The HNRDA is organized into many program/priority areas (A through beyond L). The two most relevant to Smartalyze:

### K. Artificial Intelligence (pp. 90-91)
- Explicitly names **data science, machine learning, deep learning** as priority training/capability areas.
- Notes the Philippines' relatively low 2019 Asia-Pacific AI Readiness Index score (44.2/100, 6th regionally) as the motivating gap — i.e., the country needs *more* accessible AI/data-capability tools, not fewer.
- 2023-2024 priorities specifically list "Recommender systems," "Situation interpretation through heterogeneous sensors," "Supervised, Unsupervised Learning," "Cognitive Computing" — all adjacent to what Smartalyze's rule-based cleaning + ML trend/anomaly detection + LLM-assisted explanations implement in miniature, applied form.

### J. Smart Cities (around p. 89, under "PRIORITIES FOR 2023")
- Lists **"Big Data, Data Mining, and Data Analytics"** and **"Multi-dimensional data correlation"** as explicit 2023 priorities under the DOST Smart and Sustainable Communities and Cities Framework.

**What to write:** Smartalyze aligns with the HNRDA's **Artificial Intelligence program area (Section K)**, particularly its emphasis on building national capability in data science and machine learning — Smartalyze applies these techniques (rule-based cleaning, linear regression trend detection, IQR anomaly detection, LLM-assisted explanations) in an accessible form for non-specialist users, contributing in a small, applied way to closing the AI-readiness gap the HNRDA itself identifies. It also touches the **Smart Cities priority area's "Big Data, Data Mining, and Data Analytics"** priority, since Smartalyze's core function is exactly that: enabling data mining/analytics work for users (including local students, researchers, and small business owners) who would otherwise lack access to such tools.
